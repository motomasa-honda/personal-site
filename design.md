# Design — Motomasa Honda and だんどり

The shared brand tokens in [`assets/brand/brand.tokens.css`](assets/brand/brand.tokens.css) are synchronized from the Dandori repository. This site is a personal engineer portfolio, not a company sales site: the page order is person → representative work → technical writing → other experiments → contact. Product pricing and onboarding belong on the Dandori product site.

## Shared visual rules

- Warm, light paper surfaces; do not change theme with OS dark-mode settings.
- Dark ink primary actions, with a restrained terracotta accent and separate semantic status colors.
- Shared Japanese body and display stacks, tabular numerals, warm hairline borders, and visible focus treatment.
- Compact pill navigation and rounded public actions; touch actions are at least 44px high.
- Keep motion short and honor `prefers-reduced-motion`.

## Content rules

- Write as Motomasa Honda, in a direct first-person voice.
- Describe only verified work and experience. Do not invent client results, service guarantees, availability, or technical credentials.
- Preserve existing article markdown, dates, slugs, and public URLs.
- Keep legal pages factual and do not change legal obligations as a design edit.
